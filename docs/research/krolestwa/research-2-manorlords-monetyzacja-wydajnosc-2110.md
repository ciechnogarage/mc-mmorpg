## 1. Mojang Usage Guidelines (https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines, pobrane 2026-10-21, strona bez daty "last updated"; stopka (c) 2026) - POTW (oficjalne)
Dozwolone (cytaty):
- "You may host a server for Minecraft. You may even charge for access to the server as long as you follow the guidelines in this section."
- "Selling entitlements that affect gameplay provided they don't ruin other players' experience or give a competitive advantage in the game"
- "Selling cosmetics, except for capes or anything that attempts to visually act like the feature of a Minecraft player cape"
- "Selling and using in-game virtual currencies as long as they: Have no real-world value and can't be cashed out, used, transferred across other servers, or converted into any other real-world currency; Don't give the impression that the in-game currency is from or affiliated with Mojang; Don't look like or have a name like Minecoins or any other virtual currency"
- "Asking for donations, so long as you don't offer the donor something that only they can use However, you may offer all players server wide rewards if donation goals are met."
- Dostep: "Charging for access ... The cost to access the server is the same for everyone"; dostep "Can't be limited to or controlled ... by a player owning or having access to out-of-game content".
Warunki ogolne: "All servers, entitlements, and advertising are suitable for audiences of all ages (for example, gambling, pornography, violence ...)"; "You provide any user who gives you real-world currency an online payment and purchase history"; "You provide information to users on all the content and pricing applicable to your server before a person joins"; "We reserve the right to decide if the way you are monetizing your server is consistent with our brand. In short, don't do evil things to players and you should be fine."
Mody: "The mod doesn't create a play-to-earn function where players earn real-world or out-of-game currency or in-game currency that can be cashed out".
Czego NIE ma w tekscie: slow loot box / random / subscription / battle pass / pay-to-win (sprawdzone grepem na calej stronie i na /eula). Wniosek: lootboxy nie sa nazwane wprost, ale "gambling" jest wymieniony jako tresc nieodpowiednia dla wszystkich, wiec platne losowe nagrody = wysokie ryzyko (interpretacja, BZ). Przyspieszenia/waluta premium (bez wymiany na realne pieniadze)/ranga QoL/battle pass: nie zakazane wprost; granica to "competitive advantage" i "ruin other players' experience". Przyspieszenia w trybie PvP-napadu to wlasnie ten grzyb: w PvE-symulatorze latwiej obronic, w napadach na graczy - ryzyko. Subskrypcje/ceny: wolno, ale cena ma byc jawna przed wejsciem + historia zakupow.

## 4. Wydajnosc wielu wysp
- Advanced Slime Paper = FORK Paper (nie plugin), format SRF od Hypixela; cel "load worlds faster and save space"; brak liczb producenta. https://github.com/InfernalSuite/AdvancedSlimePaper (POTW). Ma zastapic jar Papera - dla nas decyzja architektoniczna.
- Wersje: API buildow infernalsuite ma buildy 1.21.11 (49 buildow, ostatni 2026-06-01, galaz paper_upstream); main = 26.2 (commit "Promote 26.2 to main" 2026-10-15); 26.1.2 w main. Zrodlo: https://api.infernalsuite.com/v1/projects/asp + GitHub commits (POTW, przeliczone parserem). Dla 1.21.11 stabilna galaz "main" juz nie jest 1.21.11 - trzeba sprawdzic konkretny build.
- Format: zstd, brak danych generacji; chunki trzymane w RAM do rozladowania swiata; limit teoretyczny 46340x4630 chunkow; "ASP keeps a copy of all the chunks loaded in memory until the world is unloaded". https://infernalsuite.com/docs/asp/faq ; https://github.com/InfernalSuite/AdvancedSlimePaper/blob/develop/SLIME_FORMAT
- Brak publicznych benchmarkow ms-load SRF vs Anvil (szukalem; BZ). Liczby trzeba zmierzyc u nas.
- SuperiorSkyblock2 + SSBSlimeWorlds: swiat na wyspe, laduje przy teleporcie, rozladowuje po bezczynnosci; autosave moze lagowac -> `ticks-per.autosave` 6000. https://wiki.bg-software.com/superiorskyblock/overview/addons/ssbslimeworlds (1Z, producent)
- BentoBox: raport 1 usera ~10 s freeze przy tworzeniu wyspy (paste) - stara wersja, 1Z https://lightrun.com/answers/bentoboxworld-bentobox-massive-lags-upon-island-creation
- PolarPaper (Modrinth): format jak Slime bez forka, tylko plugin, wspiera 1.21.11-26.3, caly swiat w RAM, "not designed for large worlds", brak benchmarkow. https://modrinth.com/plugin/polarpaper (1Z)
- Hypixel SkyBlock: ladowanie wyspy przy wejsciu / instancje - nie znalazlem technicznego zrodla (BZ). FAWE: strona dokumentacji bez liczb (BZ). Symulacja offline matematyczna: brak zrodla w tym researchu (to wzorzec z gier mobilnych, nie sprawdzony).

## 5. Replay
- AdvancedReplay (Jumper251, 173 gwiazdki, push 2026-08-10): kod zrodlowy sprawdzony: nagrywa PAKIETY przez ProtocolLib (Client POSITION/LOOK/ENTITY_ACTION/ARM_ANIMATION/BLOCK_DIG; Server SPAWN_ENTITY, ENTITY_DESTROY, VELOCITY, EXPLOSION, MOVE/LOOK/TELEPORT) w `PacketRecorder`; `Recorder` agreguje co 1 tick w `runTaskTimerAsynchronously(...,1,1)`; odtwarzanie przez `Replayer` tez timer async, aktorzy to pakietowe NPC (`PacketNPC`) = fałszywe encje po stronie pakietow, nie realne byty. Zapis: plik / baza / S3. POTW (kod). https://github.com/Jumper251/AdvancedReplay (src/main/java/me/jumper251/replay/replaysystem/...). Brak liczb CPU/dysku.
- ServerReplay (senseiwells, mod Fabric - NIE Paper): nagrywa po stronie serwera, format Replay Mod/Flashback; odtwarza w kliencie (mod) albo serwer wysyla pakiety; "little to no impact" (deklaracja); duze: pakiety dzwiekow. https://github.com/senseiwells/ServerReplay (1Z).
