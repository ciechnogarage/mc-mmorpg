# Good example - Drzewo-Serce arena scene (level_1 build v2)

What made it work (validated by screenshots + user acceptance):

- **Anchor**: colossal Heart-Tree at (0,-22) behind the boss arena - tapered
  trunk (r4->r3->r2->pillar), 8 stepped root buttresses, glowing heart chamber
  facing the arena (air pocket + shroomlight + amethyst), sea_lantern core.
- **Crown**: 8 stepped 2x2 branch segments + multi-lobe canopy (central ellipsoid
  r14 + per-branch lobes + top knob), leaf palette with 8% air / 3% shroomlight.
- **Horizon closed**: 12 hills (r9-13, h4-6) at r=37 + 24 trees in 2 wall layers
  (r=35/40, h11-17, mixed oak/dark_oak/cherry/azalea) - island reads as a
  cathedral, zero bare skyline.
- **Contrast**: corruption wedge NE (sculk/deepslate + transition bands), sinkhole
  with catalyst/shrieker, 4 dead stripped trees, soul_fire, sculk tongue reaching
  toward the arena - tension without touching the fight space.
- **Flowers in 14 typed clusters** + 10% sparse grass (replaced 47% uniform spam).
- **Function points kept clear**: combat radius r12 flat and clean, spawn/finish
  coordinates untouched, mossy_stone ring flush in the floor.

Encounter FX that matched the bar: intro (sonic_boom + bell + glow + root burst),
enrage (roar + red dust + cherry petals), death finale (end_rod column + petals,
then levelup + bell + title "Gaj odetchnal").

Why it is good: anchor + closed horizon + contrast implement the 5-second rule;
stepped branches and clustered flora follow organic rules; FX are three discrete
scenes, not continuous particle soup; every claim was screenshot-validated.
